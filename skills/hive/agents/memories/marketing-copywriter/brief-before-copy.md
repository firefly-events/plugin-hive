The marketing-strategist produces the campaign brief. Read it completely before writing.

Key brief sections that determine copy direction:
- **Positioning statement** — sets the single message the copy must reinforce
- **Audience segment** — determines register (formal/casual), vocabulary, and emotional hooks
- **Channel plan** — each channel has different length limits, CTA styles, and tone expectations
- **Success metrics** — defines what "convert" means for this campaign; CTAs must match

Channel-specific rules to extract from the brief before writing:
| Channel | Length | CTA style |
|---------|--------|-----------|
| Email subject | ≤50 chars | curiosity hook or urgency |
| Social (Twitter/X) | ≤280 chars | action verb first |
| Landing page hero | ≤10 words | benefit statement |
| Ad body | ≤90 chars | problem → solution |

Copy written without consulting the brief almost always fails on positioning alignment in the first review pass. Reading the brief adds two minutes; a revision cycle costs 30+.
